## Reply thread, 2023-06-26

Under the original post, a reader frames the difficulty in terms of word order:
"it would take 620 quintillion guesses even if they knew the exact 24 words, but
not the correct order. No one will crack this." Keysa's reply engages with counting
permutations for the 12-word case rather than correcting the framing to "you would
first need to know which 12 words": "Well then, I reckon this strategy is a secure
way to store a seed when traveling... How many guesses would be needed if it was
done with a 12-word seed?"
