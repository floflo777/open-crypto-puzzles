# Author material, dated

## The original announcement, 2018-11-09

> "Level 5 - Find the private key in this image"

Posted with the image `crypto5.png`: [twitter.com/Zd3N/status/1060955171591766018](https://twitter.com/Zd3N/status/1060955171591766018).

## The Christmas hint bundle, 2018-12-24

> "Puzzles' Hints Xmas Bundle. <BTCrypto L5> Sum of two consecutive following
> rectangles areas creates one byte of the private key. Apply more operations to
> obtain the results in byte range. <XIXOIO> Byte 0x77 is part of the private key.
> #HPPXMS Good luck! [/64] [#12]"

Source: [twitter.com/Zd3N/status/1077146640090316800](https://twitter.com/Zd3N/status/1077146640090316800).
This bundle covers several of the author's puzzles at once; only the first sentence
("sum of two ... following rectangles areas creates one byte of the private key,
apply more operations to obtain the results in byte range") is understood to apply
to LVL5. The rest reads as bundle material for the author's other, separate
puzzles.

## The 2021 correction

The author republished the image as `crypto5fix.png` on 2021-12-12, with this note
(reproduced in the community documentation repository, see Sources):

> "UNSOLVED for over 3 years because the original release was uncomplete!
> Relaunched on 12th of December 2021. My excuses to everyone! Past hint.
> (clarity edit: sum of two consecutive following rectangles...)"

The word "consecutive" is struck through in the author's own edit, leaving "sum of
two following rectangles." No rectangle geometry changed between the two image
versions; the fix adds 2 short white pixel lines (under rectangles 40 and 53) and a
small 4-line pixel hint in the bottom-left corner reading, once corrected for a
transcription error, "-1 times x plus 64 divided by x," with "LXIV" (64 in Roman
numerals) elsewhere in the same hint.
